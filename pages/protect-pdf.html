Secure Your PDFs with AES Encryption

When sending sensitive documents via email or storing them on shared drives, you must ensure they cannot be opened by unauthorized parties. FileForge allows you to encrypt your PDFs with military-grade AES encryption directly in your web browser.

How to Password Protect a PDF

Drop your PDF into the tool and define a strong password. Our cryptographic engine will rewrite the file, encrypting the internal streams and objects. Once encrypted, the file will be completely unreadable to anyone who does not possess the exact password.

Zero-Knowledge Security

Because the encryption happens locally on your device using the Web Crypto API, FileForge operates on a zero-knowledge basis. We never see your document, and we never see the password you use to secure it. It is the ultimate privacy solution.
