Shrinking PNGs to 50KB

PNG images are typically much larger than JPEGs because they use lossless compression and support transparency. However, if you need a PNG to fit under a 50KB limit for a website or application, you need a smart compressor.

FileForge compresses your PNG down to 50KB by intelligently converting it to an optimized format like WebP or by applying lossy compression algorithms that preserve the alpha channel (transparency) while drastically reducing the file size.

Like all FileForge tools, this happens entirely offline for maximum privacy.
