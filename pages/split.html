Secure PDF Splitting Without the Cloud

Splitting large PDFs into smaller, manageable chunks is essential when dealing with extensive reports, legal documents, or e-books. With FileForge, you can extract specific pages or divide a PDF completely offline, ensuring your confidential documents remain strictly on your device.

How to Extract Pages from a PDF

To extract specific pages, upload your document and select the "Extract page range" mode. You can enter comma-separated values like "1, 3, 5-10" to pull out only the exact pages you need. The tool instantly generates a new, smaller PDF containing only your selected pages.

Instant Browser-Based Processing

Waiting for massive PDFs to upload to a remote server, process, and download again can take minutes. Because FileForge processes the split directly in your browser using WebAssembly, the entire operation happens in milliseconds, regardless of your internet speed.
